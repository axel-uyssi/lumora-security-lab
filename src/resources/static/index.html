About the Property

A sanctuary carved
into the caldera

Perched on the volcanic cliffs of Oia, Caldera Villas Infinity Resort is one of the most sought-after properties in the Aegean. Carved directly into the caldera, each villa offers an uninterrupted panorama of the sea, the sunset, and the volcanic islands below.

The resort features 12 private villas, each with its own infinity pool that appears to merge seamlessly with the Aegean. The design honors the traditional Cycladic architecture — whitewashed walls, curved forms, and an absence of excess — while delivering every modern luxury discreetly.
